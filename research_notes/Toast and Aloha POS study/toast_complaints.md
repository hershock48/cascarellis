# Toast POS: Documented Weaknesses, Complaints, Failures and Controversies (US, 2023–2026)

**Method note for the report writer.** Research was done 2026-10-26. The sandbox proxy returned 403 on every outbound page fetch (restaurantbusinessonline.com, pos.toasttab.com, support.toasttab.com, merchantmaverick.com, paymentsdive.com, bbb.org, trustpilot.com, tabski.com all refused), so no page could be read in full. Every finding below comes from search-engine result excerpts of the linked page. Quotes are reproduced as they appeared in those excerpts; treat them as near-verbatim rather than verified against the full page. ## Key Question 1: What are the top recurring complaints, with representative quotes, dates and sources?

### Takeaway
Across press, review aggregators, BBB and competitor-compiled Reddit summaries, the same seven complaints recur: (1) mandatory bundled payment processing and rate increases after signing; (2) subscription/add-on creep; (3) multi-year auto-renewing contracts with early-termination fees and proprietary hardware ("lock-in"); (4) slow, inconsistent post-sale support; (5) deposit delays and held funds; (6) outages (notably the 20 Oct 2025 AWS outage) and doubts about offline mode; (7) the July 2023 $0.99 guest fee, which crystallised a trust deficit that still colours sentiment.

### Cited Findings
- **Fees and lock-in dominate Reddit sentiment (secondhand).** A competitor summary says analysis of Reddit threads found "Toast fee increases and hardware lock-in were among the most common pain points raised by restaurant owners." — [Sleft Payments, "Toast Raised Your Fees Again?"](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/toast-pos-raised-fees-options-2026) [competitor blog]
- **"Tripling our fees" quote.** A Reddit-sourced operator quote reproduced by a competitor: "they just sent a notice that they were TRIPLING our fees." Another says Toast is "rarely the cheapest." Gathered from "Reddit threads in early 2026." — [Sleft Payments](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/toast-pos-raised-fees-options-2026) [competitor blog, Reddit links not provided]
- **Lock-in is "the most common complaint."** "Hardware is Toast-specific, and walking away before the contract expires means paying thousands in penalties... leaving Toast means losing both your POS and your processing." — [GetVMS, "Leaving Toast POS: 7 Reasons Restaurants Are Switching"](https://www.getvms.com/leaving-toast-pos/) [competitor blog]
- **Support: "very hit or miss."** Reddit-sourced quote in a competitor article; same article claims "hold times of 30 minutes or more are reported frequently during peak Friday and Saturday evenings" and that "support tickets ... go nowhere and promises to call back ... never happen." — [Sleft Payments, "Toast POS Problems & Complaints (2026)"](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/toast-pos-problems-complaints-2026) [competitor blog]
- **Capterra automated review summary (2026):** ~78% of 46 phone-support mentions negative; ~90% of 39 fee mentions negative. Phone-support complaints centre on long waits, unreturned calls and trouble reaching knowledgeable staff; fee complaints on hidden costs, billing errors and difficult refunds. "Most users report unexpected charges and difficulty getting refunds or billing corrections." — [Capterra Toast POS reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/)
- **Capterra owner quote on cost:** "The monthly fees, hidden fees, and per-item charges add up to a very expensive bill." — [Capterra reviews p.10](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/?page=10)
- **Capterra: sales vs. support disconnect.** Multiple reviewers say the sales team was far more responsive than post-sale support; one reports a bank-change request took over two months across several channels; another says support "sent requests to the wrong people and ignored follow-ups." — [Capterra reviews p.12](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/?page=12)
- **Capterra: hardware-driven churn.** One owner said they were going back to Square after Toast hardware kept failing. — [Capterra reviews p.15](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/?page=15)
- **Software Advice:** reviewers describe cost and hidden fees as "a deal-breaker"; one switched to a competitor after a trial. — [Software Advice Toast reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/retail/toast-pos-profile/reviews/)
- **Trustpilot (US):** reviews cite "unhelpful support, unreturned calls, and conflicting answers from representatives, plus deposit delays and fragmented instant deposits"; overall characterised as "mostly negative, with payment processing delays, unexpected extra fees, transaction discrepancies and hard-to-manage bank deposits." One roundup puts Toast at ~3.1/5 on Trustpilot. — [Trustpilot toasttab.com](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/toasttab.com); roundup figure via [CardPaymentOptions](https://www.cardpaymentoptions.com/point-of-sale/toast/)
- **BBB complaint volume (conflicting):** one review site says Toast holds a "B" BBB rating with 171 complaints; another roundup cites 235 BBB complaints. Neither figure confirmed on BBB's own page (fetch blocked). — [CardPaymentOptions](https://www.cardpaymentoptions.com/point-of-sale/toast/); [PaymentPop](https://paymentpop.com/merchant-accounts/toast-pos-reviews/)
- **Sonary's characterisation of complaints:** "aggressive sales tactics, lack of pricing transparency upfront, long-term contracts (often 2-3 years) that are very difficult and expensive to cancel." — [Sonary Toast POS review 2026](https://sonary.com/b/toast/toast+pos/)
- **Payment Review's common-complaint list:** refund-related financial issues, billing transparency, and support communication gaps; lists "No third-party processor allowed" as a con. — [Payment Review](https://paymentreview.com/reviews/toast-pos/)
- **Account-takeover reports, July 2025 (secondhand).** "In July 2025, multiple restaurateurs shared on Reddit that their entire Toast POS system had been hijacked. An attacker got in, changed their login credentials, and effectively hijacked the digital nerve center of their operation"; support "was slow to respond." Same post notes no platform-wide breach disclosed by Toast. — [Flyght, "Burnt Toast"](https://www.whatisflyght.com/blog/burnt-toast-when-your-pos-becomes-your-biggest-vulnerability) [competitor blog; includes a fictional composite "Rusty Copper Tavern" example]
- **Toast's own community forum: scam callers impersonating Toast tech** in response to real support tickets. — [Toast Community thread 18511](https://community.toasttab.com/t5/back-office-team/cautioning-others-hackers-claiming-to-be-toast-tech-calling-in/m-p/18511) [official forum]; Toast's help centre confirms "an active phishing scam involving emails impersonating Toast" from lookalike domains and recommends MFA on both email and Toast accounts. — [Toast Support, "Protect Your Toast Account From Scammers"](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Protect-Your-Toast-Account-From-Scammers) [official]
- **Trust narrative from the 2023 fee episode.** Matt Wilhelmson, Koehn Bakery (Missouri): "I feel like [Toast] showed us that we can't trust them to be a business partner." Owners called the fee "medieval" and "unethical." — [Fox Business, July 2023](https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/restaurateurs-fuming-ominous-new-fee-billed-customers-big-tech-vendor) [press]

### Inferences
- The complaint pattern is structural, not feature-level: the three things operators say they hate most (bundled processing, auto-renewing term, proprietary hardware) are the three things that make Toast's unit economics work. A challenger that offers processor choice, month-to-month terms and commodity hardware attacks the complaints directly.
- Support complaints are consistent from 2019-era Capterra reviews through 2026 competitor compilations, suggesting no step-change improvement is visible to operators.
- The account-takeover and phishing reports imply operators want stronger default account security (mandatory MFA, out-of-band verification of "Toast support" calls, audit logs of credential changes).

### Gaps
- No Reddit thread could be retrieved directly; every Reddit quote is as relayed by a competitor blog without a link, thread title or date. Thread titles/dates requested in the brief could not be verified.
- BBB complaint count and rating unverified (page fetch blocked; two sources give 171 and 235).
- No evidence found on whether support is offshore; no data on account-rep churn.

## Key Question 2: What does Toast actually cost a small independent per month and per transaction once everything is added?

### Takeaway
Published list prices are $0 (Starter Kit) or $69/mo (Point of Sale), but processing is mandatory and quoted per restaurant (roughly 2.49% + $0.15 in-person on paid plans, ~3.09–3.69% + $0.15 on the $0 plan, ~3.50% + $0.15 card-not-present), and add-ons typically push a single location to $150–$500/mo in software before processing; one competitor worked example lands at $23,292/yr all-in.

### Cited Findings
- **Base plans:** Starter Kit $0/mo; Point of Sale $69/mo; Build Your Own custom-quoted. — [NerdWallet Toast review](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/reviews/toast-pos); [Merchant Maverick pricing guide](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/toast-pricing-guide/)
- **Processing rates:** 2.49% + $0.15 in-person "if you buy hardware upfront"; 3.09% + $0.15 "pay-as-you-go option"; NerdWallet range 2.49–3.69% + 15¢ in-person vs Square 2.4–2.6% + 15¢. — [NerdWallet best restaurant POS](https://www.nerdwallet.com/p/best/small-business/restaurant-pos-systems); [NerdWallet Toast vs Square](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/learn/toast-vs-square)
- **Card-not-present (online orders):** 3.50% + 15¢. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/); [Loman pricing breakdown](https://loman.ai/blog/toast-pos-pricing) [competitor blog]
- **Rates not printed on Toast's pricing page**; quoted per restaurant on volume, average ticket and card mix. — [Restaurantify](https://restaurantify.com/toast-pos-pricing-explained/) [third-party blog]
- **Add-on list prices (third-party compilations, figures conflict):** Online Ordering ~$75/mo; Loyalty $50–75; Gift cards ~$50; KDS software $25–35 plus hardware; Marketing ~$75 (one source $185); Payroll $6/employee or $90 + $9/employee; third-party delivery integration ~$30/platform or $75. — [Loman](https://loman.ai/blog/toast-pos-pricing); [POS USA pricing](https://www.posusa.com/toast-pos-pricing/); [Dineopen](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/how-much-does-toast-pos-cost-2026); [Restaurantify](https://restaurantify.com/toast-pos-pricing-explained/)
- **Typical total:** "Most single-location restaurants pay about $150 to $500/month for software and add-ons, before processing"; online ordering + loyalty + KDS + gift cards reaches $200–400/mo, "which is why some sources say add-ons can double the bill." — [POS USA](https://www.posusa.com/toast-pos-pricing/); [Certus AI operator review](https://www.certus-ai.com/blogs/toast-pos-review)
- **Hardware:** ~$449–$1,024 upfront, or $0 upfront on Starter Kit at the higher processing rate; Flex terminals "starting at $799+". — [Loman](https://loman.ai/blog/toast-pos-pricing); [Restaurant Armor](https://restaurantarmor.com/blogs/toast-pos-handheld-solutions/toast-pos-pricing-the-real-cost-why-protection-matters)
- **Worked annual example:** "Toast POS Can Cost $23,292/yr" (competitor's full-stack example). — [Sleft Payments](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/toast-pos-pricing-vs-independent-processing-2026) [competitor blog]; a second competitor claims an operator could "Save $7,800 in 2026" by leaving. — [Sleft Payments](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/toast-pos-raised-fees-options-2026)
- **Processing rate increase, Sept 2024 [press]:** Toast "quietly raised its processing fees for its small and mid-sized restaurant clients by between .05% and .23% in September," per a William Blair research note; Toast said the increase "only applies to a limited number of clients" and that it "had never increased card processing rates for customers in the prior 12 years." Toast simultaneously planned to roll out a surcharge feature to more clients; analyst did not expect churn. — [Payments Dive, 9 Oct 2024](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/toast-processing-fee-increase-restaurants-surcharge-feature/729315/)
- **Surcharging:** effective 1 Dec 2024 for restaurants that set it up; debit/prepaid cannot be surcharged. — [Merchant Cost Consulting Toast review](https://merchantcostconsulting.com/lower-credit-card-processing-fees/toast-review/); Toast FY2024 10-K says it offers "automated, industry compliant credit card surcharging to help eligible customers offset their card processing fees." — [SEC 10-K FY2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1650164/000165016425000072/tost-20241231.htm) [official]
- **Contractual rate escalation:** "Toast reserves the right to increase its credit card processing fees at any time during the contract (with 30 days advance written notice), and some customers have reported this happening." — [Merchant Maverick Toast review](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/reviews/toast-pos-review/); "If you do not accept the change in writing before the effective date, continued use counts as acceptance." — [StartupOwl](https://startupowl.com/reviews/toast)
- **Renewal-time increases (unverified):** one guide claims contracts "generally permit annual increases of 5% to 10%" and that "many operators renewing in 2026 are seeing increases of 15% or more compared to rates signed in 2023." — [Checkthat.ai Toast pricing](https://checkthat.ai/brands/toast/pricing) [aggregator, unsourced]
- **No processor choice / proprietary hardware:** "You cannot use a third-party payment processor or bring your own hardware." — [StartupOwl](https://startupowl.com/reviews/toast); "Mandatory Toast payment processing, with rates that vary by deal." — [PayKings](https://paykings.com/blog/toast-pos-review-your-2025-guide-to-stress-free-restaurant-management/)
- **Line-by-line fee catalogue (2026):** — [Labrador AI, "Toast POS Fees in 2026"](https://www.labrador.ai/blog/toast-pos-fees-2026) [competitor blog]; [Merchant Insiders, "Toast Fees Explained"](https://merchantinsiders.com/blogs/toast-fees/)

### Inferences
- A plausible all-in for a $1M/yr single-location independent on Point of Sale + Online Ordering + KDS + Loyalty + Gift Cards: ~$270–$300/mo software (~$3,300–3,600/yr) plus ~$27,000–30,000/yr processing at ~2.6–3.0% blended, i.e. the processing line is ~8–10x the software line. This is an inference from the list prices above, not an operator-reported figure.
- The Sept 2024 rate increase broke Toast's "12 years without an increase" record and gave competitors a durable talking point; operator anger is about the 30-day unilateral-change clause as much as the basis points.

### Gaps
- No operator-reported invoice or worked example from a named restaurant was found; the $23,292/yr example is a competitor's construction.
- Add-on list prices conflict between sources and Toast's own pricing page could not be fetched; treat all add-on figures as approximate.
- No official Toast notice of a 2025 or 2026 processing-rate increase was found despite competitor claims of "another round."

## Key Question 3: What happened with the $0.99 customer fee in 2023, step by step, and what did it reveal?

### Takeaway
Toast added a non-optional $0.99 "order processing fee" paid by guests on Toast-hosted online orders of $10+, disclosed only inside a combined "Taxes & Fees" line; it went nationwide 10 July 2023, triggered press, owner revolt and a House Small Business Committee inquiry, and was withdrawn 19 July 2023 with CEO Chris Comparato saying "We made the wrong decision." It showed operators will not tolerate a vendor monetising their guests without consent or an opt-out, and that trust, not the dollar amount, was the issue.

### Cited Findings (chronological)
- **24 June 2023 [press]:** Boston Globe reports Toast will add a 99-cent fee to online orders over $10, "starting at a small set of restaurants and going nationwide later." The fee "was labeled only as part of a combined 'Taxes & Fees' line" and "was only identifiable if a customer tapped a small 'i' icon." "The many restaurants Toast works with have already been notified, and some are outraged." Toast "did not offer an option to allow restaurants to cover the fee themselves." Toast's defence: the fee "helps fund product investments and continued innovation in support of helping restaurants maintain the direct relationship with their guests." Analyst Eugene Simuni: reflects "the pressure they are feeling to get to profitability." — [Boston Globe via boston.com, 24 Jun 2023](https://www.boston.com/news/the-boston-globe/2023/06/24/new-toast-fee/)
- **23 June 2023:** ConsumerAffairs frames it as a "junk fee" hitting customers of 85,000 restaurants and bars. — [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/one-more-junk-fee-is-hitting-customers-of-85000-restaurants-and-bars-062323.html) [press]
- **Fee mechanics:** paid to Toast, not the restaurant; applied to online orders of $10 or more through Toast's online ordering; restaurants "were not given a choice to opt out." — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/toast-remove-99-cent-fee-after-widespread-backlash) [press]; [Hospitality Technology](https://hospitalitytech.com/toast-drops-99-cent-fee)
- **10 July 2023:** fee rolled out nationwide. — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/toast-remove-99-cent-fee-after-widespread-backlash)
- **Mid-July 2023 [press]:** Fox Business reports restaurateurs "fuming"; owners call fees "medieval" and "unethical"; Koehn Bakery's Matt Wilhelmson: "I feel like [Toast] showed us that we can't trust them to be a business partner." — [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/restaurateurs-fuming-ominous-new-fee-billed-customers-big-tech-vendor)
- **Within 48 hours of the Fox report:** Rep. Mark Alford (R-Mo.), for the House Small Business Committee: "We are going full steam ahead with investigating the propriety of their way of doing business." — [Fox Business, congressional query](https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/sudden-fees-charged-millions-americans-big-tech-vendor-toast-spark-congressional-query) [press]; the episode "fueled threats of class-action lawsuits." — [Fox Business, mom-and-pop revolt](https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/mom-pop-restaurant-revolt-forces-big-tech-vendor-toast-rescind-fees-american-diners)
- **19 July 2023 [official/press]:** Toast announces removal by end of week. Comparato: "We made the wrong decision and following a careful review, including the additional feedback we received, the fee will be removed from our Toast digital ordering channels." He added the company "would still adjust pricing thoughtfully to help fund product investments." — [Toast, "Update on Toast Digital Ordering for our Customer Community"](https://pos.toasttab.com/news/update-on-toast-digital-ordering-for-our-customer-community) [official]; [Boston Globe, 19 Jul 2023](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2023/07/19/business/toast-drops-99-cent-online-order-fee-after-pushback-restaurants/); [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/toast-remove-99-cent-fee-after-widespread-backlash); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/restaurant-technology/2023/toast-reverses-course-on-0-99-consumer-fee-following-restaurant-outcry/); [QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/growth/fast-casual/toast-shuts-down-its-99-cent-fee/); [Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/toast-software-processor-customer-online-order-fee-payment/688361/)
- **Stock reaction:** down "nearly 12% early Wednesday" (RB); another report says shares fell "more than 16%." — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/toast-remove-99-cent-fee-after-widespread-backlash); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/toast-inc-removes-hidden-fee-after-outrage-from-customers/456156)
- **Industry read: merchants pay anyway.** Reforming Retail headline: "Toast to Remove Order Fee by End of Week, But Toast Merchants Will Ultimately Pay Up." — [Reforming Retail, 19 Jul 2023](https://reformingretail.com/index.php/2023/07/19/breaking-toast-to-remove-order-fee-by-end-of-week-but-toast-merchants-will-ultimately-pay-up/)
- **August 2023 earnings call:** Comparato called the fee a "mistake" and acknowledged Toast "erred in how it approached monetizing the value of Toast's online ordering services." — [Payments Dive, "Toast CEO: Lessons learned from fee flub"](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/toast-ceo-lessons-learned-fee-flub-restaurant-pos-payments-chris-comparato-elena-gomez-aman-narang/690509/)
- **Leadership change linked by press:** Fox Business headline: "Big Tech restaurant vendor Toast to replace CEO after fee outrage caused by 'breach of ethical leadership'" (Comparato succeeded by Aman Narang, effective 1 Jan 2024). — [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/big-tech-restaurant-vendor-toast-replace-ceo-fee-breach-ethical-leadership) [press; causal framing is Fox's]
- **Residual variant:** one review source reports "a reduction to $0.49 for some merchants" rather than full removal in some cases. — [CardPaymentOptions](https://www.cardpaymentoptions.com/point-of-sale/toast/) [unverified]
- **Timeline summary with lessons:** — [Craver, "From Rollout to Recall"](https://localbites.craverapp.com/toast-99c-fee) [competitor blog]

### Inferences
- Operators' objection was threefold: no consent, no opt-out/absorb option, and disclosure buried in "Taxes & Fees" so guests blamed the restaurant. A challenger should hard-code: no vendor-imposed guest fees, and any pass-through fee is operator-controlled and itemised.
- Comparato's "we will still adjust pricing thoughtfully" presaged the Sept 2024 processing increase and the Dec 2024 surcharge feature: the revenue was recovered from merchants rather than guests.

### Gaps
- Outcome of the House Small Business Committee inquiry not found.
- No class action over the fee was found to have been filed.
- Exact pilot start date (pre-24 June) and share of locations in the pilot not confirmed; one source says pilot "at a portion of its restaurant client locations," another dates the fee to June 2022 (likely an error).

## Key Question 4: What are the contract and exit terms, and what do operators say about trying to leave?

### Takeaway
Toast contracts typically run 2–3 years, auto-renew for one-year periods unless 30 days' written notice is given, and the early-termination fee is essentially all remaining software subscription fees for the term (or ~$150/remaining month on pay-as-you-go), plus hardware return obligations; operators describe surprise renewals and ~$1,600 ETFs, and there is no clearly documented data-export policy.

### Cited Findings
- **Term and auto-renewal:** "The contracts can be as long as three years and renew automatically after the initial term for another year. If you want to get out of your contract at any time, you'll have to pay an e[arly termination fee]." — [Merchant Maverick Toast review](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/reviews/toast-pos-review/)
- **Notice window:** "requires at least 30 days' written notice before the term ends to avoid renewal." — [PaymentPop](https://paymentpop.com/merchant-accounts/toast-pos-reviews/); "the termination fee accelerates whatever is left of the term, so the day the renewal window closes is the day your exit cost resets to twelve months." — [weAudit Toast rating 60/100](https://www.weaudit.com/ratings/toast/)
- **ETF formula:** "remaining software fees for your term, plus any financing-related processing charge." — [Merchant Maverick](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/reviews/toast-pos-review/); "$150 per remaining month" on pay-as-you-go; "a restaurant closing after one year on a 2-year contract could owe $1,000+ in ETF alone." — [StartupOwl](https://startupowl.com/reviews/toast); competitor range "$1,000 to $5,000+" when hardware financing is included. — [Sleft Payments](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/toast-pos-pricing-vs-independent-processing-2026) [competitor blog]
- **Historical ETF clause (2018):** fee computed from the highest monthly fee multiplied by months remaining. — [Reforming Retail, "Are Toast's Early Termination Fees Usurious or Expected?" (Sept 2018)](https://reformingretail.com/index.php/2018/09/11/are-toasts-early-termination-fees-usurious-or-expected/) [historical]
- **Public example of a signed US order form (March 2023)** showing "Software Subscription Total" structure. — [Helotes EDC signed Toast contract PDF](https://helotesedc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Toast-contract-signed-3_2023-8398bb78dd526a24beeca9b1e8327555.pdf) [primary document; contents not read]
- **Australian merchant agreement [official, non-US]:** ETF "is due on top of the remaining software fees for the current term." — [Toast Merchant Agreement Australia](https://pos.toasttab.com/au/merchant-agreement)
- **Rate-change exit conflict:** weAudit says a merchant who terminates after a rate increase "does not pay the termination fee"; Merchant Insiders warns "the early termination fee may still apply." — [weAudit](https://www.weaudit.com/ratings/toast/) vs [Merchant Insiders](https://merchantinsiders.com/blogs/toast-fees/)
- **Hardware on exit:** Toast "typically requires you to return hardware, settle outstanding balances, close your account, and transfer customer data"; "improper packaging could lead to damage fees ranging from $200 to $500 per item." — [Lavu, "How to Cancel Toast POS Contract"](https://lavu.com/how-to-cancel-toast-contract/) [competitor blog]; hardware "doesn't carry over if you leave"; "Only works on Toast-approved hardware." — [Perfect Venue](https://www.perfectvenue.com/post/toast-restaurant-software)
- **BBB complaint on auto-renewal:** a customer disputes "a roughly $1,600 early termination fee after Toast treated the contract as renewed for a year" following a 24-month term "that was not clearly flagged as auto-renewing." — [BBB Toast complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/ma/boston/profile/point-of-sale-systems/toast-inc-0021-164973/complaints)
- **Law360 (2024):** "Restaurant Says Toast Refusing To Update Bank Info" — a restaurant alleged Toast would not change its deposit bank account. — [Law360](https://www.law360.com/corporate/articles/1891203/restaurant-says-toast-refusing-to-update-bank-info) [press; paywalled, details not read]
- **Data export:** no official Toast export policy found; competitor guidance: "you can export most of your data (sales reports, customer info, menu items), but some historical data may not transfer seamlessly to a new system"; advises requesting "average ticket, transaction count, and card mix" before leaving. — [Sleft Payments](https://www.sleftpayments.com/learning-hub/toast-pos-pricing-vs-independent-processing-2026) [competitor blog]
- **Reporting export limits (relevant to exit):** Capterra reviewer: order details "cannot be exported to Excel for pivot tables"; Toast-hosted guide: custom reports and filters "cannot be saved or easily printed." — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/); [Toast Tips PDF](http://d2c9w5yn32a2ju.cloudfront.net/knowledgebase/Toast-Tips-New-POS-Guide.pdf)

### Inferences
- The combination of (a) 30-day unilateral rate changes, (b) 30-day pre-expiry notice to avoid a 12-month auto-renewal, and (c) ETF = remaining term, is the mechanism behind "lock-in" complaints. A challenger that publishes a one-page exit policy (month-to-month after year one, full data export in open formats, hardware stays with the operator) would convert this directly.
- Absence of a public data-portability page is itself a weakness operators notice only when leaving.

### Gaps
- Current US Merchant Agreement text could not be fetched; ETF formula and whether rate-change exits waive the ETF remain unresolved between sources.
- No official Toast statement on what data (menus, guest/loyalty records, historical sales) can be exported, in what format, or for how long after termination.

## Key Question 5: How does support perform, and has it changed since 2023?

### Takeaway

## Key Question 6: What are the documented outage incidents, and how did offline mode hold up?

### Takeaway
The best-documented incident is the 20 Oct 2025 AWS us-east-1 outage (~15 hours), during which many Toast restaurants reported "complete system failures," could not run cards and fell back to paper, while Square stayed largely up via multi-region architecture; Toast's own offline mode keeps dine-in orders and (if "background card processing" is on) card capture working but halts online/third-party orders, menu edits/86ing, and shifts offline-decline risk to the merchant.

### Cited Findings
- **AWS outage impact [press]:** "many restaurants on the Toast point-of-sale system experienced complete system failures, disrupted scheduling systems, problems with waitlist management, and other issues." A Virginia restaurant said it cost "significant business"; "JD's Hamburgers in Fort Worth, Texas, manually added its transactions after its Toast system went down"; "A manager at Cattleman's Roadhouse in New Albany, Ind., offered to pay for meals because the restaurant was unable to process credit cards." — [Nation's Restaurant News, "The AWS outage left many restaurants scrambling"](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology/the-aws-outage-left-many-restaurants-scrambling)
- **Duration and redundancy contrast [press]:** "The 15-hour outage was bad for many restaurant brands"; Square "operates some of its key products across multiple AWS regions, so that if one region goes down... those systems can stay up"; another vendor said its POS stayed up. — [Restaurant Business, "For restaurant tech, AWS outage puts spotlight on redundancy"](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/restaurant-tech-aws-outage-puts-spotlight-redundancy)
- **Offline mode behaviour [official]:** devices switch to Offline Mode automatically on loss of internet, local network or Toast cloud; banner appears "after about 40 seconds." Still works: new orders on POS and Toast Go; "Accept credit and debit card payments and capture tips, as long as background card processing is enabled. Background card processing is on by default." Does not work: "Menu changes — including 86ing items and price overrides via Quick Edit — cannot be made while in Offline Mode"; reports exclude offline devices until they sync. — [Toast Support, "Use Toast in Offline Mode"](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Using-Toast-in-Offline-Mode); [Toast Central Offline Mode Overview](https://central.toasttab.com/s/article/Offline-Mode-Overview-1492794307880)
- **Offline card liability [official]:** "Restaurants are responsible for offline transactions that are later declined, expire, or are disputed. Pre-authorizations only work if the card was pre-authorized before the outage began." — [Toast platform docs, Offline mode](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformOfflineMode.html); [Offline mode overview](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminOfflineModeOverview.html)
- **KDS offline dependency [official]:** kitchen tickets keep flowing only "if the network has Universal Plug and Play multicast enabled and at least one hardwired Toast device acts as the local hub." — [Toast platform docs](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformOfflineMode.html)
- **Online ordering during outages [official]:** "Your Toast devices will not receive any orders placed from your online ordering page"; orders placed during the outage arrive once reconnected. — [Toast Support, Outages: Online Ordering](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Outages-and-Disruptions-Toast-Online-Ordering); [Third-Party Ordering](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Services-and-Disruptions-Affecting-Third-Party-Orders)
- **Toast Go handheld offline limits** (third-party explainer). — [Maple, "Toast Go handhelds"](https://maple.inc/blog/toast-go-handheld) [competitor blog]
- **Operator view of offline mode:** Capterra reviewer: "when Toast's cloud goes down, owners can't do much," and Wi-Fi drops cause problems; another says "some features don't work offline." Reviewers cite "outages at peak hours" and "doubts about offline mode." — [Capterra p.17](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/?page=17); [p.18](https://www.capterra.com/p/136301/Toast-POS/reviews/?page=18); [Sonary](https://sonary.com/b/toast/toast+pos/)
- **Counter-view:** G2 comparison says reviewers see offline mode "as a major advantage for keeping service running during network outages." — [G2 Toast vs TouchBistro](https://www.g2.com/compare/toasttab-com-vs-touchbistro-restaurant-pos)
- **Status tracking:** third-party trackers exist; Toast tells merchants to check its status page first and "not restart equipment during a disruption." — [StatusGator Toast](https://statusgator.com/services/toast); [IsDown Toast](https://isdown.app/status/toast); [Toast Support, Troubleshoot Internet](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Outages-and-Disruptions-Troubleshoot-Internet-or-Network-Connection-Issues)

### Inferences
- The AWS incident shows the gap between "offline mode" (designed for a restaurant losing its own internet) and a cloud-side outage where devices may still have internet but no backend; some restaurants evidently could not run cards at all, implying either background processing was off, devices had not entered offline mode, or dependent services (scheduling, waitlist, online ordering) failed regardless. A challenger should design true local-first operation with store-and-forward payments and explicit testing for cloud-down-but-internet-up.
- Shifting decline risk for offline transactions to the merchant is standard for the industry but is a source of post-outage chargeback complaints.

### Gaps
- No Toast post-incident report or official statement on the 20 Oct 2025 outage was found.
- No catalogue of other Toast-specific outages 2023–2026 (dates, durations) was found; status-page history could not be fetched.
- Exact offline limits (max offline transaction amount, max hours before forced sync) were not visible in excerpts.
