# Comparable deals and buyers in US restaurant technology, 2021 to 2026

Scope note: US-focused, 2021 to 2026, with a few pre-2021 and non-US deals included where they set a reference price (Lightspeed/Upserve 2020, Resy 2019, Zonal/Volaris 2026, TheFork 2026). Figures are labeled as **reported** (company release or SEC filing), **press-reported** (trade or financial press citing sources), **aggregator** (PitchBook/CB Insights/Tracxn/GetLatka/Sacra style databases, which often conflict), or **my arithmetic** (a multiple I computed from two sourced figures). Several trade sites (restaurantbusinessonline.com, restauranttechnologynews.com, multiples.vc) could not be fetched directly from this environment, so for those the search-result excerpts are the basis.

## Key question 1: What did each named company raise or sell for, when, and what were its locations, revenue or GPV at the time?

### Takeaway
The two restaurant-tech IPOs of 2021 (Toast at ~$20B, Olo at ~$3.6B) marked the peak; since then the sector has seen one PE take-private at ~7x trailing revenue (Olo), a run of strategic acquisitions priced between $10M and $1.2B, and two distressed exits of once-well-funded POS vendors (TouchBistro ~$100M, Upserve up to $81M). Only a handful of private companies (Owner.com, SpotOn, Restaurant365, Deliverect) have raised at $1B+ since 2022, and Owner.com is the only one with a 2026 up-round at a disclosed valuation.

## Key question 2: Who are the active acquirers of small restaurant-tech companies and what have they paid for companies with under $10 million in revenue?

### Takeaway
The active buyers split into four groups: payments processors buying POS for payments volume (Shift4 above all, plus Fiserv/Clover and Block), restategics buying features for their platform (Toast, Olo, DoorDash, PAR, GoTab, ChowNow, Lunchbox), card networks buying dining distribution (American Express), and permanent-capital software consolidators and PE (Constellation's Harris and Volaris, Thoma Bravo, Corsair, Skyview). Disclosed prices for sub-$10M-revenue targets are rare; the clearest data points are Toast/Delphi at $10M and Olo/Omnivore at $49M, with most small deals undisclosed.

### Inferences
- The recurring rationale for buying a regional or niche POS is the installed merchant base and the payments volume attached to it (Shift4's pattern), not the software itself; buyers then migrate merchants onto their own POS. A Michigan register/ordering platform would be evaluated the same way: locations, GPV, and attach rate.
- Niche feature companies (drive-thru, loyalty, back-of-house, enterprise ordering) get bought by the platform whose customers already use them as an integration partner (Spendgo/Olo, GoParrot/Square, Cuboh/ChowNow); being a top integration partner is a visible path to acquisition.

## Key question 5: What is the current state of restaurant-tech venture funding in 2025 to 2026 compared with 2021?

### Takeaway
Restaurant-tech VC fell from nearly $12B globally in 2021 to about $1.3B in 2024 (PitchBook), then rebounded in dollar terms in 2025 through a few very large rounds and buyouts while deal counts kept falling; by H1 2026 foodtech deal counts hit an eight-year low with capital concentrated in late-stage, AI-branded companies such as Owner.com.

### Cited Findings

- 2021: nearly $12 billion of VC flowed into restaurant tech globally, per PitchBook — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/why-investors-are-pouring-billions-restaurant-technology); broader foodtech VC was $49B in 2021 vs $10.6B in 2024 — [PitchBook Q2 2025 foodtech](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q2-2025-foodtech-vc-trends)
- 2024: restaurant-tech VC totaled $1.3B, down from a $14.5B peak in 2018 per PitchBook's category definition (note the 2018-vs-2021 peak discrepancy between sources) — [The Spoon](https://thespoon.tech/report-restaurant-tech-funding-drops-to-1-3b-in-2024-but-ai-automation-provide-glimmer-of-hope/); [Consumer Equity Partners](https://consumerequitypartners.com/news/2026/08/14/5/); foodtech deal count hit a record low in Q4 2024 — [Fortune/PitchBook](https://dc.fortune.com/2025/02/21/foodtech-deal-count-hit-a-record-low-in-q4-2024-says-pitchbook)
- 2025 by quarter (PitchBook foodtech, restaurant tech as a segment): Q1 foodtech $1.4B over 202 deals — [PitchBook Q1 2025](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q1-2025-foodtech-vc-trends); Q2 foodtech ~$2B over 128 deals, with restaurant & retail tech $1.4B across 35 deals — [PitchBook Q2 2025](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q2-2025-foodtech-vc-trends); Q3 foodtech $2.8B, restaurant tech 56.7% ($1.6B), and restaurant tech 43.4% ($4.1B) of trailing-twelve-month capital — [PitchBook Q3 2025](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q3-2025-foodtech-vc-trends); Q4 foodtech $2.5B over 128 deals — [PitchBook Q4 2025](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q4-2025-foodtech-vc-trends)

### Inferences
- Dollar totals in 2025 look healthier than 2024 only because of a handful of mega-rounds and buyouts (Owner.com, SevenRooms, Olo); seed and Series A counts are at multi-year lows, so a small studio should expect a thinner early-stage market than in 2021 and more interest from strategics and consolidators than from VCs.
- The capital that is flowing favors companies with disclosed $100M+ ARR and an AI narrative (Owner.com, MarginEdge's "AI back office"), not undifferentiated POS.
